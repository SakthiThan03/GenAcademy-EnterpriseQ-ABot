# Travel and Expense Policy
Document ID: FIN-003 | Owner: Finance | Updated: 2026-08-20
## Travel authorization
Business travel requires manager approval before booking. International travel also requires Finance approval.
## Reimbursements
Submit itemized receipts through the expense portal within 30 days after the expense. Meals are reimbursable up to $75 per day during approved business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
## Lodging
Hotel expenses up to $220 per night are reimbursable without additional Finance approval. Higher rates require written preapproval.
## Personal devices
Personal device purchases are not reimbursable unless IT and Finance approve them in advance.
