# Data Retention and Privacy Standard
Document ID: PRIV-006 | Owner: Privacy Office | Updated: 2026-10-01
## Retention
Customer support tickets are retained for 24 months after closure. Recruiting applications are retained for 12 months after the hiring decision. Finance expense records are retained for seven years.
## Deletion
At the end of the retention period, approved deletion workflows remove data unless a documented legal hold applies.
## Data access
Access to customer personal data is role-based and must be justified by a business need. Suspected unauthorized disclosure must be reported to Security within one hour under the IT Security Policy.
## External sharing
Customer personal data may only be shared with approved processors under a signed agreement. Consumer AI tools are not approved processors.
