# New Employee Onboarding Guide
Document ID: OPS-005 | Owner: People Operations | Updated: 2026-09-20
## First day
New hires must complete identity verification, tax forms, and direct deposit setup in the HR portal. IT issues a company-managed laptop and sets up corporate accounts.
## First week
Complete security awareness training and enable multi-factor authentication before accessing internal systems. Review the Employee Handbook, Code of Conduct, and IT Security Policy.
## First month
Meet your manager to establish 30-day goals. For planned time off, follow the Employee Handbook PTO request process.
## Support
Contact People Operations for HR questions and IT Helpdesk for account or device access.
