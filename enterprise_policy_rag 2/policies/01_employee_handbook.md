# Employee Handbook
Document ID: HR-001 | Owner: People Operations | Updated: 2026-09-01
## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. New hires accrue PTO monthly from their start date. Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least five business days before planned leave. Managers approve requests based on staffing needs.
## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Notify your manager before your shift when possible. Medical details are not required in the general team chat.
## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees working remotely must follow the IT Security Policy, including device and VPN requirements.
## Parental leave
Eligible full-time employees with six months of service receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Contact People Operations at least 30 days before the anticipated start when practical.
