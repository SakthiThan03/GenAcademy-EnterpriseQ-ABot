# IT Security Policy
Document ID: IT-002 | Owner: Security | Updated: 2026-09-15
## Authentication
All employees must use multi-factor authentication for corporate accounts. Passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Password sharing is prohibited.
## Devices and remote access
Remote workers must use company-managed devices with disk encryption and automatic screen lock after five minutes. Access to internal systems from outside the office requires the corporate VPN.
## Incident reporting
Report suspected phishing, stolen devices, or compromised credentials to security@asterworks.example within one hour of discovery. Do not investigate suspicious links yourself.
## Data classification
Confidential customer records must be stored only in approved company systems. Never send confidential records to personal email or consumer AI tools.
