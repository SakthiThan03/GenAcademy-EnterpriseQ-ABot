# Code of Conduct
Document ID: LEG-004 | Owner: Compliance | Updated: 2026-07-10
## Respectful workplace
Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, and bullying are prohibited. Employees can report concerns to People Operations or the confidential ethics channel.
## Conflicts of interest
Disclose financial interests in vendors or customers to Compliance before participating in related purchasing decisions.
## Gifts
Employees may accept business gifts worth no more than $50 per giver per year. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts are never permitted. Gifts exceeding the limit must be declined or reported to Compliance.
## Confidentiality
Protect confidential business information and follow the IT Security Policy for storage and transmission.
